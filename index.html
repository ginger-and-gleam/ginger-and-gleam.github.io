Services

Three ways we care for a home.

Every home starts with a walk-through and, for ongoing care, a First Visit that brings everything to baseline. After that, regular visits keep it there.

✦

Recurring care

Weekly, every other week, or monthly. Kitchens, baths, floors, dusting, beds, and the small finishes that make a house feel settled when you walk in.

✦

The First Visit

A top-to-bottom deep clean: baseboards, fans, vents, blinds, grout, inside the oven and refrigerator. The start of ongoing care, or a seasonal reset on its own.

✦

Move-in and move-out

An empty home, cleaned the way you'd want to find it: inside every cabinet, drawer, closet, and appliance. For closings, listings, and new keys.

How it works

Clear price. No guessing.

1

A fifteen-minute walk-through

We come by, see the home, and ask a few questions: which lights you like on, what should never be moved, the surfaces that need care, how the dog feels about visitors.

2

A written price, the same day

One message with the First Visit price and the per-visit price for ongoing care. Add-ons are listed separately, so nothing is a surprise.

3

Your First Visit

The home comes down to baseline. A handwritten note is left on the counter, and you'll get a message the moment we're done.

4

Then, quietly, every visit after

The same careful hands, the same standard, the same finishes. If you'd ever like something done differently, just say so; it's noted for every visit after.

The experience

Cared for, not just cleaned.

No rotating crews and no strangers with a key. Your home is looked after by the same careful hands each visit, and your preferences are remembered from one visit to the next, so you only have to say it once.

Stone, wood, brass, and fine finishes are treated with the right products. Lights, blinds, and thermostat are left exactly as you like them.
