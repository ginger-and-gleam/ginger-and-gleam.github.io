About the owner

I started Ginger & Gleam because I knew there was a better way to clean a home.

Before opening my own business, I spent years in the industry as a cleaner, a trainer, and in quality control, and I learned how the operations side works as well. I’ve trained other cleaners, inspected their work, and helped shape how jobs get done, so I know exactly what a high standard looks like and what it takes to meet it. But my standards were always higher than what the schedule allowed. Larger cleaning companies run on tight windows and rotating crews, and there was never room to do the work the way I believed it should be done. I wanted to give homes the attention they deserved, so I built a business around that.

You’ll never meet a stranger at your door. Every visit, it’s the same person in your home, and I learn it the way you do. I know which surfaces need a gentler product, which cabinet you’d rather I leave alone, and that the vase in the hallway was a gift. I know your dog’s name, and which corner of the couch is his.

That familiarity is what sets this service apart. Because I’m in your home visit after visit, I notice the details others miss: the baseboards, the grout lines, the inside of the glass shower door, the way you like your pillows arranged. Your preferences are remembered, never repeated. Whether you prefer fragrance-free products or a certain order to the work, I’ll do it your way every time without being asked.

To me, luxury isn’t excess. It’s being cared for by someone you trust in your home. That’s my promise: meticulous work, quiet professionalism, and the time to do it right.

Good to know

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients are at work during their visit. Entry details are shared privately by text and never written down.

Will it be the same person every visit?

Yes. No rotating crews and no strangers with a key. Your preferences are remembered from one visit to the next, so you only have to say it once.

What do you use on stone, wood, and brass?

Products made for each surface. Everything is brought to the home; if you'd prefer we use yours, or you'd like fragrance-free products, just say so.

Is the inside of the oven or refrigerator included?

They're add-ons on every service, quoted at the walk-through. Move-outs include the dishwasher, microwave, and laundry machines.

How is the price set?

By the size of the home and how often you'd like care. We send a range the same day you message us, then confirm it in writing after the walk-through. Add-ons are listed separately.

Why start with a First Visit?

Regular care keeps a home at a standard; the First Visit sets it. Baseboards, vents, fans, and grout are brought back once, so every visit after can keep them there. If your home is already in excellent shape, we'll tell you at the walk-through.

How do I pay?

Venmo, Zelle, or cash. We send an invoice at the end of the day.

What if I need to cancel?

Let us know as early as you can. Same-day cancellations are $50, waived for a true emergency.
