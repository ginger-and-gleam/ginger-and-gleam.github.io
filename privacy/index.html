Privacy

Ginger & Gleam Cleaning Co. is a residential cleaning business in Hutto, Texas. This page explains what we collect when you contact us and how we use it.

What we collect

  • What you send us: your name, email, mobile number if you give it, your city, roughly how big your home is, the kind of care you'd like, the days that suit you, and any notes.
  • This can reach us through the request form on this site, Facebook Messenger, email, or a Facebook or Instagram request form.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, arrange a walk-through, send your quote, and schedule and invoice your visits.
  • We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't add you to a mailing list.
  • Entry codes and alarm details are shared privately by text and are never written down.

Services that carry your message

  • The form on this site is delivered to our email by FormSubmit.
  • Messages and request forms on Facebook and Instagram are handled by Meta under its own privacy policy.
  • Our email is hosted by Google.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep your details for as long as we're caring for your home, or until you ask us to delete them. To see, correct, or delete what we hold, email gingerandgleam@gmail.com.
