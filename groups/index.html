Step 1

Newest posts asking for a cleaner

Each search shows the most recent Facebook posts first, including posts in your groups.

Step 2

Your groups

Paste a group's link once (open the group, copy the address from the browser bar). It's saved on this device, and each group gets its own "cleaner" search.

Step 3

Reply

Pick an opening so it isn't the same comment every time, copy, paste, then type @Ginger & Gleam and choose the Page so it's tagged.

  • No prices, no phone number, no discounts in public comments. Ranges go in Messenger.
  • If a client recommended you in the thread, thank them by name.
  • Still reply if others are already tagged. A polished reply stands out.
  • Any lead from a group goes in your Sunday text as "FB group."
