The Home
Care Guide

Your home, detailed by hand.

Ginger & Gleam Cleaning Co. · Hutto, Texas

I.

It begins with a walk-through.

Fifteen minutes in your home, at a time that suits you. It is how we learn the house before we ever clean it.

  1. 01

    We see the home

    Room by room, with you or on your own schedule.

  2. 02

    We ask what matters

    Which lights you like on, what should never be moved, the stone or wood that needs a gentler product, how the dog feels about visitors.

  3. 03

    Your quote, in writing, the same day

    Every home is quoted personally, so you never pay for a package built for someone else's house. The First Visit and your ongoing care arrive in one message, with any additions listed separately.

II.

The First Visit sets the standard.

Think of it as paint correction for the house. Regular care keeps a home at a standard; the First Visit sets it, so every visit after can keep it there.

Detailed by hand

  • Baseboards
  • Door frames and tops
  • Ceiling fans
  • Air vents and returns
  • Blinds
  • Grout lines
  • Light switches
  • Faucet bases
  • Every room, top to bottom

Inside the oven and refrigerator can be added. If your home is already in excellent shape, we'll tell you at the walk-through.

III.

Then, quietly, every visit after.

Weekly or biweekly care, with monthly visits on request. The same careful hands each time, on a schedule you keep.

Kitchen

Counters, backsplash and cabinet fronts wiped down; the sink and faucet polished; appliance exteriors and the stovetop cleaned.

Baths

Showers, tubs and glass; toilets, vanities and mirrors; fixtures brought back to a shine.

Living and bedrooms

Dusting within reach, beds made, the small things set back where you keep them.

Floors and finishes

Vacuumed and mopped with products chosen for each surface; the last look under the light before we leave.

Laundry, fresh linens, interior windows, inside cabinets, garage and patio can be added to any visit.

IV.

You only have to tell us once.

No rotating crews and no strangers with a key. The same person cares for your home every visit, and your preferences are remembered from one visit to the next.

Home notes

Entry lamp on when leaving.

Fragrance-free in the primary bath.

Leave the office as is.

Marble: stone-safe products only.

Entry details are shared privately by text and are never written down.

V.

Good to know.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients are at work during their visit.

What do you use on stone, wood and brass?

Products made for each surface, all brought to the home. If you'd like us to use yours, or prefer fragrance-free, just say so.

Where do you work?

Within about thirty minutes of Hutto: Round Rock, Pflugerville, Georgetown, Taylor and north Austin. Weekdays, nine to five.

How does payment work?

Venmo, Zelle or cash. An invoice arrives at the end of the day.

What if plans change?

Let us know as early as you can. Same-day cancellations carry a fee, waived for a true emergency.

What if I'd like something done differently?

Just say so. It's noted for every visit after.
